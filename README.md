# Bar POS

Bar POS is a mobile point-of-sale app for bartenders. It helps staff take orders, keep track of sales, and serve customers using the stock assigned to their counter.

## What you can do

- Sign in with your phone number and PIN.
- Browse products available at your assigned counter, search by name, or scan a barcode.
- Build an order, adjust item quantities within available stock, add a discount or notes, and place an order on hold.
- Complete checkout with cash or mobile money. For cash, the app calculates the change due.
- Review completed orders and view a daily shift summary with sales totals.
- Refresh counter information when an internet connection is available.

## Designed for day-to-day service

The app is designed for Android phones and portrait use. Product and order information is kept on the device so staff can continue working with previously downloaded counter data when the connection is unavailable. When the app cannot refresh bartender or counter information, it offers a retry or a choice to continue with available local data.

Completed orders are saved on the device. Uploading orders to a server is not currently available, so order records remain local.
